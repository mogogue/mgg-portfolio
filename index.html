I enjoy building things.
So I build.

Got an idea? In weeks, not months, I turn it into a working prototype backed by a database, so you can test it with real users and see whether it's really worth it before investing in development. AI speeds things up, but as a Product Owner, I decide what to build and why it matters.

What I do

  • Understanding the idea

    The idea and what sits underneath it: what it really solves, and for whom. Getting that right shapes everything else.

  • Building & AI Prototyping

    I make your ideas into something you can touch and test. Working prototypes: AI makes it faster, I decide and bring the judgment.

  • Tech & UX Translator

    Curiosity led me to learn enough tech and enough UX: to ask well, lean on the team, and make the right calls when I build alone.

  • Team Alignment

    What we build, why, and where we're heading. And the how: a team that enjoys it builds better.
